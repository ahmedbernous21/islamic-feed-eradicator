Islamic Feed Eradicator

A Chrome extension that replaces distracting feeds with sourced reminders. These pages describe how it handles information and how religious text is chosen.

Privacy Content policy Source

Privacy

Settings, favorites, and personal notes stay on your device. The extension does not send browsing history or page contents to a server.

Read the privacy policy

Content policy

Reminders come from a bundled library. Community suggestions are checked against a source before they can be published. Missing context is left blank rather than invented.
